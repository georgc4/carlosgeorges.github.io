03 / ABOUT

A few layers
down.

I started in computer engineering because I wanted to understand the machine, not just the interface. That curiosity has taken me from RTL and physical design to Linux kernels, developer infrastructure, and a word game.

At Intel, I now work on Linux platform and pre-silicon enablement: kernels, boot flows, graphics, images, and validation that help other engineers use the hardware. Before that I spent four years in physical design and EDA, where I worked on GPU power connectivity and shared implementation flows.

Outside work, I keep taking on projects that make me cross a boundary I don’t fully understand yet. An ARM64 hypervisor fix landed in m1n1; an original RISC-V SoC now boots Linux in simulation. The best part is usually the point where the layers finally line up.
